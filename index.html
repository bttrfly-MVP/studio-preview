Questions

A few things to know

What is bttrfly Studio?

Studio is bttrfly’s web-based platform for businesses. It brings profile management, family conversations, and tools for discovery and engagement together in one workspace.

Who is Studio for?

Studio is built for family-centered businesses, brands, and providers—from childcare and enrichment to wellness, household services, travel, and more.

Why is my business already on bttrfly?

We’ve added business profiles to help families discover relevant resources. Claiming yours lets you update your information, personalize your profile, and receive messages from families.

How does Emmy help families discover my business?

Emmy uses business profile information to identify relevant options for a family’s request. A complete, up-to-date profile helps her understand what you offer. Emmy discovery is available through the paid plan.

How do families contact me?

Families can message you through your claimed business profile in the app or on the web. You receive and reply to those messages in Studio.

Can I get started for free?

Yes. You can claim or create your profile, update your core business information, and reply to family messages for free. Additional discovery, lead management, and insight tools are available through the paid plan.
