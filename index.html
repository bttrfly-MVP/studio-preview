Questions

What businesses ask us.

Is it really free?

Yes. Claiming your page, keeping it up to date, and answering families' messages are all free. Founding Membership is $9.99 a month and adds Emmy introductions, tour requests, leads, email alerts and insights.

Why is my business already on bttrfly?

We've added many local family businesses so parents can find them in the app. Your page can't receive messages until you claim it, so claim it to take it over, correct anything, and start hearing from families.

Who are the families on bttrfly?

Parents in New York City who use the bttrfly iPhone app to find care, classes and services, ask Emmy everyday questions, and connect with other parents nearby.

How does Emmy decide who to recommend?

Emmy introduces claimed Founding Members whose page matches what a parent asked for, and ranks businesses closer to the family higher.

How do families contact me?

From the message button on your page, in the app or on the web. Messages arrive in Studio, where you reply. You get an alert in the bttrfly app on every plan, and Founding Members also get email alerts and a leads board.
